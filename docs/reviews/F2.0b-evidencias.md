```text
VMO ... 350 kt
MMO ... M 0.82
(A320, MSN 01767-09045) CONF 1 230 kt | 1+F 215 kt | CONF 2 200 kt | CONF 3 185 kt | FULL 177 kt
(A321, MSN 08557, 08587, 08655, 08710, 08768, 09063) CONF 1 243 kt | 1+F 225 kt | CONF 2 215 kt | CONF 3 195 kt | FULL 186 kt
Maximum speed with the landing gear extended (VLE) ... 280 kt /M 0.67
Maximum speed at which the landing gear may be extended (VLO extension) ... 250 kt /M 0.60
Maximum speed at which the landing gear may be retracted (VLO retraction) ... 220 kt /M 0.54
The load factor is automatically limited to: +2.5 g to -1 g for clean configuration.
```
Dente do sim = posição da alavanca (1 = CONF 1, 2 = CONF 2, 3 = CONF 3, 4 = FULL), conferido com a captura (A320neo: 230/200/177). N1/EGT não usados: este FCOM é da frota PW1100G; o A320neo do MSFS usa outro motor.
